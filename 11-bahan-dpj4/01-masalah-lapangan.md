## 5. Empathy / perspective-taking layer (why the device is more than a trainer)

- **Core claim (user requirement):** when a hearing person practices signing with the device, they experience — concretely, with their own hands and body — the visual-manual communication mode that deaf people use all day. The device is therefore an empathy instrument, not only a vocabulary trainer. `ID-READY seed: "Latihan isyarat yang dimediasi perangkat menempatkan pembelajar pada posisi yang selama ini dihuni tunarungu: mengandalkan gerak visual sebagai sarana utama berkomunikasi."`
- **Support available:** Ritmeester 2026 (L1) documents how demanding the auditory→visual shift is for hearing learners; K2 shows the direction of burden (deaf side adapts). Rodríguez-Correa 2023 (K4) frames technology as part of the answer in education settings.
- **Honest boundary:** do NOT claim the device "creates empathy" as a measured outcome — a 1-semester feasibility study will not measure empathy scales. Position empathy as the **design intent / expected benefit**, cite K1–K4 for the burden asymmetry. A small usability pilot (03 §7) may report learner experience qualitatively.
